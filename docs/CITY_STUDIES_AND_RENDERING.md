# City studies and renderer optimization

- Adds a physical five-stop sketch walk after the letter, clerk and Mara errands. The East Avenue sign starts or cancels the walk; Civic Row, South Park, Canal Walk and River Plaza supply four studies. Completion gives one art layer (if unfinished), two inspiration and two work points once. Navigation suspends for urgent story events or a tracked hike. Site coordinates, artwork and markers follow the generated map; the secret terrain tools remain unchanged. A checksummed six-byte per-slot companion record at 0x300 preserves progress without rewriting existing save formats.
- The original facade painter scaled each pair of columns at runtime. An offline generator now produces 128 exact sheared indexed wall rasters for the eight shipped/common heights, styles and orientations. The contiguous row copier uses aligned word transfers, with byte-safe clipping and the original arbitrary-height fallback. The 98,048-byte pixel atlas and 766 row-span bytes reside in ROM, not work RAM.
- Outdoor rendering acquires the terrain mesh once per frame. The isometric view uses four vertex loads for its center and corners, and perspective uses exact precomputed side/scale tables for all 2,480 ray samples, preserving the original bilinear rounding and signed division. Neither camera adds a framebuffer.
- Added facade golden cases covering every actual district height and clipping/alignment combinations, 184,320 bilinear samples across all survey styles, exact perspective offset tests, and 960 sign-location checks plus full live sketch walks across four representative world seeds. These tests are in the regular host suite. Cartridge memory and FPS will be measured by CI rather than inferred from theoretical work reductions.
