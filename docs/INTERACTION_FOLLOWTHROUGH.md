# The Last Days of Danny Steel — Interaction and narrative follow-through

This branch builds on the tested full-game playability milestone on `main`
without replacing its shortest-route finder, four-page notebook,
30-VBlank attack tell or save data.

The practical interaction improvement fixes the genuine last-bus ambiguity:
when Danny follows his waypoint to a standing tile beside both the bus and
the music desk, A selects the current objective unless Danny deliberately
faces the other actor. A real guided walking test verifies the path and
confirms that the bus conversation, not the music console, opens.

Five short, two-page observations give distinct voices to the Potheads
block, market, canal, north quarter and old pavement. They are optional
writing—not fake quest steps or hidden relationship scores. Danny remains
grateful for the job's stability without pretending to love wearing the suit.

The B button now advertises an ethical-risk option only where the confirmed
risk menu is really available. FACTS gains one focus (maximum four) for a
perfectly timed same-lane parry, while CARE retains its original first-mistake
shield; the game's readable 30-VBlank strike warning stays intact.

No new framebuffer, heap allocation, save-state field, collision change,
music table or storyboard asset is added. The existing larger campaign is
still under development and is not claimed to be perfected by this pass.
