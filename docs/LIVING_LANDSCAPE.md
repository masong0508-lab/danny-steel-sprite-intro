# Connected landscape foundation

The city is now connected through six eastern gateway cells to a 24x30 rural region, for a 64x30 world. The existing story anchors remain in place. A deterministic generator uses curved trail segments, elliptical terrain fields with bounded slopes, irregular woodland and a natural lake. The heightfield is sampled continuously in world coordinates and used for uphill walking and outdoor camera elevation. The isometric landscape uses sloping triangles and the first-person landscape uses near-to-far heightfield spans. Existing urban raycasting and rendering remain intact. No loading screen or separate expansion menu connects the areas; the rural painter currently changes at the city boundary and the visual handoff still needs capture/tuning.

Visit the marked survey plots at (44,8) or (54,24). A raises and B lowers one of four local grades. The two edits are independent and soften into their surroundings; collision and routes are not changed. A CRC-protected six-byte record per save slot keeps the edits and older saves default to grade one. Land shaping is limited to two small plots, not an unrestricted city builder. The high ridge at (56,11) provides outdoor art studies that advance the existing four-stage canvas. The shore at (43,22) offers rest. Optional activities yield to urgent story stages. These locations are in the normal world, not a DLC menu.

Generator validation: 1,351 reachable walkable tiles from the original spawn, only the same two old sealed archive tiles isolated, maximum sampled adjacent grade difference 14 across all sixteen combinations, summit height 75 projection units. New host tests exercise actual input, save corruption, ascent cooldown, project cap, map bounds, and both native camera renderers. The previous CI failure came from the old 24x18 guide-map test dimensions; the test now follows the actual map bounds and verifies its markers.
