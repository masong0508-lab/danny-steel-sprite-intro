# Studio and campaign
Walk to the music desk in the east plaza (19,7). START, L, RIGHT opens the field guide's goal picker. Choose Music Studio and A, then follow the waypoint. First visit retrieves the demo; A again offers mixing, listening/soft mix, or Studio. The basic editor has Song, Notes and Live pages, L/R switch, D-pad edits, SELECT plays the real PSG arrangement, START saves a take, B returns without advancing. Four takes unlock six-tab Advanced Studio. Sharing the finished master with Mara adds a once-only saved bond benefit; completing the conversation clears the tracked sharing goal. The field guide also tracks rooftop art and Mirror Orchard or restores the original main story; urgent events always override optional tracking. The original ending requirements and SRAM layout remain unchanged. Separate interiors expansion PR #53 is not merged here.

The completed-song conversation selects the player's saved PSG arrangement as its actual music cue. First-person storefront artwork remains unchanged to retain the independent pixel-equivalence regression; the contextual HUD identifies the studio in both cameras.

## Mixer and direct navigation
START then A opens the goal picker directly. Basic now has SONG/NOTES/MIXER/LIVE; Advanced adds ORDER and LYRICS. Select an instrument with LEFT/RIGHT, change volume with UP/DOWN, A mutes/unmutes. SELECT plays or truly stops the edited song. Leaving the editor saves edits immediately. The completed desk's Listen option plays the saved PSG arrangement.

## Cafe exhibition (seven-stage optional campaign)
After the letter, BORE stamp and promise to Mara, choose CAFE SHOW in START/A destinations, or follow the bus's Stay suggestion. Plan with Mara; design a poster at the photo shop; complete the four music takes and rehearse the actual saved phrase order; finish four paint layers and select a frame; set up at the annex; return to the cafe to perform the saved arrangement with visible poster and canvas; file the completed show at BORE. Mistakes in rehearsal are retryable, and B cancels without spending progress. The seven steps and both aesthetic choices persist within the existing story save byte; a completed show cannot farm rewards. Resume main story from the picker whenever desired. Urgent medical and rescue goals override exhibition tracking. The show reuses existing native environments; no new film-cache allocation or separate game mode.
