# Completed projects remain playable

A completed master recording formerly made the numbered-day MUSIC objective impossible: the advanced LISTEN action never called the daily-memory routine. LISTEN now records the day's music memory once, restores one energy and spark (bounded), and leaves the four saved takes and work untouched. Choosing or leaving the advanced editor is not automatically credited. Repeat listening still plays the master without farming memories.

Finished paintings retain their daily VIEW/REST options. Once the daily artwork memory is recorded, the rooftop offers a separate REST/LEAVE interaction. Rest restores one capped energy, without additional money, spark, project progress, or memory credit. The waypoint now explains that rest and exploration are optional until the next call.

Native and legacy menus describe the actual choices. Urgent appointments and rescue priorities retain their existing gates. No map, SRAM schema, Story size, renderer cache or framebuffer changes.

Regression coverage extends the existing daily-moments test to visit the real music and art stations with completed projects, exercise cancel and editor exit, earn the fifth memory and recap, rest to the cap, and retain the appointment lockout.
